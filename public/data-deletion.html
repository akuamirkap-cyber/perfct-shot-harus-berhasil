User Data Deletion Instructions

Perfect Shot · Gun Hit (com.amirkap.perfectshot)

Perfect Shot · Gun Hit does not require account registration or store passwords. Most gameplay progress is stored strictly on your device.

Option 1: Delete Data Directly in the Game

  1. Open Perfect Shot · Gun Hit.
  2. Tap SETTING from the main menu.
  3. Select RESET PROGRESS to wipe all local scores, coins, weapons, and consent settings.
  4. You can also uninstall the application to remove all local app data from your device.

Option 2: Request Deletion of Analytics / Attribution Identifiers

If you consented to analytics on Android (GameAnalytics / Adjust / Facebook App Events) and want any associated device identifiers deleted or unlinked, please email us with the subject Data Deletion Request — Perfect Shot:

Email: akuamirkap2@gmail.com

Include your approximate play date and your device's Advertising ID (optional, found in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads) if you want third-party attribution logs purged. We respond within 7 business days.
