Privacy Policy

Perfect Shot · Gun Hit (com.amirkap.perfectshot)
Last updated: 14 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Perfect Shot · Gun Hit handles game data, analytics, advertising, and your privacy rights.

1. Game Progress Stored on Your Device

Scores, coins, diamonds, unlocked weapons, quests, daily rewards, and settings are stored locally on your device (localStorage / WebView sandbox). You can erase local progress at any time inside the game under SETTING → RESET PROGRESS.

2. Consent First (GDPR)

On mobile app builds where GDPR applies, the game asks for your consent before initializing any analytics or advertising SDK. If you decline, no third-party analytics or ad SDKs are initialized, and you can continue playing locally.

3. Third-Party Services (Mobile & Portal Versions)

Depending on the platform and your consent choice, the app may use:

  • GameAnalytics — anonymous gameplay progression events (such as stage started, completed, or failed) to improve game balance.
  • Adjust & Facebook App Events — install attribution and campaign measurement.
  • Google AdMob / CrazyGames SDK / Poki SDK — optional rewarded video ads that only play when you choose to watch for an in-game bonus.

4. Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your real name, phone number, contacts, precise GPS location, photos, microphone, or camera data. The game contains no chat, friends list, or user-generated content uploads.

5. Data Deletion & Contact

To delete local data immediately, open the game and choose SETTING → RESET PROGRESS, or uninstall the app. For data deletion instructions or privacy inquiries, see our User Data Deletion page or email us at:
